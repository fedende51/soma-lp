# Scheduler — Make room for change

DECISION: Product mode, 30 seconds, 60 fps, 1920×1080 and independently composed 1080×1920. A short launch film fits the requested product story; vertical is recomposed for feed viewing.
DECISION: Audience is production planners and precision manufacturing owners. The desired feeling is confidence in changing a plan without losing control.
DECISION: English on-screen copy, no voiceover. The visual cause and effect must survive muted playback. Japanese localization can follow a native terminology review.
DECISION: SOMA's supplied light/dark PNGs are used unchanged inside SVG crop windows. Blue/cyan brand spectrum and local IBM Plex Sans / Manrope fonts follow the existing approved site assets.
DECISION: Pure frame-derived HTML/SVG with critically damped springs, rendered in Chromium and encoded with FFmpeg. No timers, stateful simulation or remote fonts in the film.
DECISION: Unattended execution, including independent critic reviews. The pasted brief explicitly says to decide blank fields and run builders in parallel; its gates are review artifacts rather than requests for repeated approval.
DECISION: Original locally composed instrumental score plus tactile synthesized UI effects. Runway music generation was attempted and returned paid_plan_required; no track was generated there. Local composition avoids making the film dependent on an account upgrade.
DECISION: End on the supplied SOMA mark, Scheduler, 'Make room for change.' and 'Plan your next move.' No public product domain was supplied or verified, so no invented URL is printed.

## Product truth

Arch/scheduler_brd.md is the source. This is the P1 planner-maintained scheduling board: direct drag, downstream impact preview, explicit confirmation, protected pinned work, reusable part routings and order-owned routing snapshots. No live machine data, operator reporting, ERP integration, multi-user collaboration or invented performance statistics.

The live Scheduler application is not in this workspace. Film panels are bespoke illustrative product visualizations built from the documented behavior, not a claimed recording or exact reconstruction of its current UI. OR-025 / OR-026, 24 pieces, machines and all clock times are explicitly labeled example data.

## Treatments considered

1. **Make room for change — selected.** A single blue operation block expands into the planning world, follows a human drag, reveals its dependencies, then becomes reusable routing knowledge. Direct visual proof of the customer's central problem.
2. **From the whiteboard.** Physical marker strokes become digital lanes. Rejected because reenacting the old tool consumes time needed to show the product.
3. **Knowledge in motion.** Start inside a part routing and grow into a factory schedule. Strong engineering story, but change management is the first priority in the BRD.

Three signature moments: a single operation opens a whole machine plan; a moved step pulls a visible path around an immovable commitment; the same path folds into the next order's reusable routing.
