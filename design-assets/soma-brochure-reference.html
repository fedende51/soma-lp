Production scheduling

In most shops the production plan is spread across a whiteboard, a spreadsheet and a stack of paper travelers. It works, but it is easy to lose track: when something changes, every document has to be updated by hand; forecasting new orders is guesswork; and finding out what ran three weeks ago means going through the paper. Soma Scheduler brings the whole plan into one place. Orders are scheduled in seconds, changes update everything they affect, and every operation — past and future — is a click away.

Plan orders visually on a timeline

Soma Scheduler shows machines, orders and deadlines on a timeline covering several weeks. Each machine has a row with its shifts, planned maintenance and scheduled work. The calendar respects working hours and holidays, so the plan is always realistic. Zoom from hour-level detail to a full month.

Schedule orders in seconds

Orders are placed with drag-and-drop. Work can only land on machines able to run it, and always in the correct sequence — the checks happen automatically. Automatic scheduling plans a single order or the whole backlog in one action, and a repeat order takes two clicks.

Changes update the whole plan

When an order is moved or changed, Soma Scheduler reschedules everything it affects — the order's remaining steps, its inspections, and any other orders that are displaced. All consequences of a change are presented for approval before anything is committed, and every action can be undone.

Forecast and keep deadlines

After every change, each order's projected completion is compared to its deadline, and orders that would be late are flagged until resolved. A capacity overview shows free and overloaded machines weeks ahead — useful when quoting new work, or when deciding whether the floor needs another machine.

Knowledge & Records

One place for process knowledge and production records

Your process knowledge, stored once

Each part's process — steps, compatible machines, cycle and setup times, inspections, and outsourced operations such as plating — is recorded once in the part library. Every future order reuses it, so scheduling no longer depends on what one person remembers or on retyping a paper card.

Every operation on record

The schedule is a record as well as a plan. Scroll back to any past day and see what ran, on which machine, for which order — with the same detail as the current plan. Tracing an operation from three weeks ago takes seconds, not an afternoon in the filing cabinet.

Live tracking with Soma Data Feed

Connect Soma Data Feed — cameras and sensors on your machines — and the schedule shows actual starts, stops and progress in real time. The plan and the shop floor on one screen, with no manual reporting.
